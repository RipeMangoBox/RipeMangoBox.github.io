Jingzhong Lin (林靖众)

I am a Ph.D. student at East China Normal University (ECNU), where I am privileged to be advised by Prof. Gaoqi He. I received my M.S. degree in Computer Science from ECNU in 2023.

My research interests lie at the intersection of Computer Vision and Computer Graphics, with a primary focus on multi-modal conditioned human motion modeling.
